About Draft 385 | 385風について

Draft 385 began as a zine produced by the members of the Winter 2023 MCC385 “Advanced Studies in Translation” class at ICU. We have since branched off into an independent student organization on campus that works to support ICU students in the pursuit of both literary and nonliterary artistic work. The first issue of Draft 385 was born in a translation seminar, and as such, our roots will always be in language and translation, but we welcome submissions of creative work in all forms. We have conceptualized the zine as a “roughly bilingual” zine of English and Japanese works that include poetry, prose, art, translation, and other (possibly multimedia) works. Whether we are monolingual or multilingual—whether we are native speakers, heritage speakers, or second-language learners of a particular language—our knowledge of language is dynamic, constantly changing in response to our experiences and perceptions. The assumption of two entirely distinct sides, in translation or in the “bilingualism” of the ICU campus, is no longer adequate to describe the complexities of our linguistic and cultural identities. Draft 385 aims to be a space where the variety of languages on our “bilingual”—but really multilingual—campus can meet and intertwine, a place where we allow literature and art to explore and redefine those boundaries, carving our own landscapes of language and culture. Draft 385 will be published twice a year, once at the end of ICU’s winter term, and once at the end of the spring term. The spring issue features open submissions from members of ICU and beyond, while the winter issue focuses on (but is not limited to) work produced by students of MCC385. Follow us on Instagram @icu_draft385 to see what we’re up to, get a sense of the types of work we publish, and stay tuned to any events (talks, workshops, readings, and more)! Additionally, if you have any questions, feedback, or ideas for a future edition, feel free to email us at icu.draft385@gmail.com.
